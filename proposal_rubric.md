#### Proposal rubric (week 4)

Criterion | Points | Strong | Adequate | Weak
---|---|---|---|---
**Problem and idea** | 4 | Clear task tied to a real user or situation. Targets a gap that current systems handle badly. Uses Arabic data or serves Arabic speakers, or a non-Arabic idea is clearly stronger. | Task is clear but the user or gap is generic. A non-Arabic idea with no strong case for it. | No real user. A standard model on a standard benchmark.
**Why it is needed (positioning)** | 4 | Two or more real systems named and their shortfall shown. Says what the team does differently. | Systems named but the shortfall is only claimed. | Fewer than two systems or no evidence of a search.
**Dataset plan** | 4 | Name, hours, licence and link for each dataset. Consent plan if recording people. Held-out test set. | Source named but key details missing. | No concrete data or no held-out test set.
**Evaluation** | 2 | Metric fits the task and success is defined. | Metric named but not justified. | No useful metric.
**Baseline** | 2 | Named pretrained model and a specific planned improvement. | Baseline named but the improvement is vague. | No baseline or an unsuitable one.
**Team, compute, and risks** | 4 | Clear roles, research-vs-engineering stance, hardware, main risk with a fallback. | Some of these are thin or missing. | No roles, compute plan or risk.
